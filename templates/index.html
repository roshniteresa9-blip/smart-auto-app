ABOUT SMART AUTO

Smarter Shared Mobility

Smart Auto is a digital shared-mobility platform designed to make everyday auto-rickshaw travel more convenient, connected and efficient. It connects passengers and drivers through a single platform with real-time trip information and simple digital boarding.

📍

Route Based Travel

Passengers can find autos operating on routes that match their destination.

🪑

Live Seat Information

Available seats are updated as passengers board the active trip.

📱

QR Boarding

Passengers can scan the trip QR code to board the selected Smart Auto.

🚗

Driver Management

Drivers can manage routes, trips, passengers and completed trip history.
